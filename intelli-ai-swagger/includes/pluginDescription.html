IntelliAI Swagger is an IntelliJ IDEA plugin that uses AI to generate and maintain Swagger/OpenAPI annotations and related documentation.

⚡ Powered by IntelliAI Engine

Key Features

  • 🧩 Swagger Annotations: Generate or update method/parameter/response annotations
  • 📦 Schema Completion: Maintain DTO/Schema annotations referenced by APIs
  • ⚙️ Provider Selection: Reuse IntelliAI Engine provider settings
  • 🔔 Notifications: Unified notifications and status bar quick entry

IntelliAI Swagger（中文简介）

IntelliAI Swagger 是一款基于 AI 的 Swagger/OpenAPI 注解生成与维护插件，帮助接口文档持续保持完整与一致。

  • 🧩 注解生成：生成/更新接口方法、参数与响应的注解
  • 📦 Schema 补全：维护接口引用的 DTO/Schema 注解
  • ⚙️ 服务商配置：复用 IntelliAI Engine 服务商配置
  • 🔔 通知与入口：状态栏快捷入口与统一通知提示
